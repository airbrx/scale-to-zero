# Probe All You Want: the storyboard

The plan for `story.js` and the score, beat by beat, against `script.md`.

**The idea.** The CDN isn't a wall. It hands out the same answer, "move
along", to anyone, for next to nothing. The protection is that there's
nothing behind the page to take hold of: a hand reaching through the door
closes on fog.

## The four questions

**The place.** A street at night, in fog, folded out of the page.
- Their lot: a full-stack shop with every door a scanner hopes for: the
  `wp-admin` front door, a key under the mat (`.env`), a shed (`.git`), a
  cellar hatch (the data), a mail slot (`mailer.cgi`), a chimney (the
  runtime). Knock and something inside wakes: windows light, the chimney
  smokes, the bill runs.
- Our address: a house front printed on a single sheet standing on the
  pavement, its door cut into the paper. Beside it, a counter handing out
  the same printed slip to every caller, and a box of finished pages.
- In the middle of the street, a notice board: the Certificate
  Transparency log. Our sheet gets pinned up; the bots come out of the fog
  to read it.

**What we follow.** One bot: from the board to the shop's front door,
the mat, the shed, the mail slot, on the narrator's words.

**The spine.** Two receipts hanging at the right edge of the frame.
Theirs runs long and red as the shop wakes, is robbed and scales; ours
barely moves.

**The rhyme.** The same night, rewound at our address. The shop folds
down into the page, cold, leaving dashed outlines where its doors were.
Its callers cross the street to our counter and get the same slip as
everyone else.

**The numbers live on a phone.** Every figure lands on a paper-cut phone
that slides in at the left edge, newest on top, with its source, among our
own access log and a few keywords: 50 bots (this site's log), seconds
(EuroS&P 2023), 4×, 2,969,010,478, 3,804,232 and 52% (GreyNoise), 110,000
and 7,000 (Unit 42), 1 → 8 (a stack we ran), $0.00. The picture never
carries a floating number; the big lines ("It was your compute", "Flat
isn't zero", the close) stay full-frame.

**The ends.** It opens on our house unfolding alone in the fog, its door
opening, the bots arriving. It closes on the same house with its door
open, callers wandering in and out: have an open house; just don't hand
out the keys.
