# How We Make These: the script

A short film about how the Report's films are made, made the way STYLE.md
says to make them. It doesn't describe the method; it does it, in the same
folded-paper material as the films themselves: one page that folds into a
street, a mill, a city, and back to the page.

It opens cold on the manifesto's ten rules (content/manifesto.json) applied,
one at a time, to a running application, until almost nothing is running.

Grammar as in README.md. Each beat's first direction names the STYLE.md
move it shows.

## 00 The cold open

> Move: the cold open, a hook. An application stands running on the page: a warehouse lit and smoking, app towers, a shiny new one, ropes out to vendors, and its bill hanging long and red at the edge. The manifesto's ten rules ink in one by one; with each, a piece of it folds down or changes, until a single printed page stands where it was.

> ♪ rules

{rules} Ten rules. {app} One application. {rebuild} Rebuilt against them, one rule at a time, {zero} until almost nothing is left running.
