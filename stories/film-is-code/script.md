# The Film Is the Code: the script

From the Report's "The film is the code" (October 2026). The receipt is a
weight: this film as an 83 MB video file and as a 1.3 MB letter of
instructions. The place is a street of readers' houses with a
depot (the CDN) at the end of the road. A video play is a lorry carrying a
crate; a drawn play is a bot carrying an envelope, and the reader's own
browser folds the film out of it on the table.

Grammar as in README.md. Each beat's first direction names the STYLE.md
move it uses.

## 07 Instructions

> Move: the other side of the ledger. A piano roll on the page; a script sheet with one line fixed; the street with one depot and no towers; the depot folding flat.

> ♪ ledger 1

{score} The music is never recorded. {roll} It's a key, a tempo and some chords, played by the reader's machine, {clock} on the script's clock.

{typo} Fix a typo, and one text file changes. {nothing} Nothing is re-rendered, re-uploaded or re-encoded. {own} Every file comes from the same place as the article, {idle} and when nobody's watching, it costs nothing.
